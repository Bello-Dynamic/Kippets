## 🌍 What is Konnexu?

Konnexu is a modern community platform designed to help people build spaces where conversations, creativity, and communities thrive.

Think:

* Forums
* Social communities
* Photo sharing
* Blogs
* Video sharing
* Activity feeds
* Private messaging
* Reactions, pokes & interactions
* Member profiles
* Custom branding

Built by community webmasters with 15+ years of experience building online communities.
